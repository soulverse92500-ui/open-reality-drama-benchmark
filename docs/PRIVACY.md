# Privacy and data policy

ORDB's bundled fixtures are synthetic. Contributors must not submit real names,
addresses, account identifiers, private conversations, unpublished production
scripts, authentication material, or analytics tied to an identifiable person.

Before publishing a run:

1. inspect every input and output file;
2. remove credentials and local filesystem paths;
3. replace names and locations with fictional values;
4. confirm that the source material is owned or appropriately licensed;
5. keep private prompts and business logic outside the repository.

The local evaluator makes no network requests.
